## My favorite Series : Summer I Turned Pretty
I love this series because of the drama, romance, and summer vibe. The characters grow a lot through the episodes.

**Genre:** Teen Romance/Drama
**Created by:** Jenny Han
**Platform:** Amazon Prime Video

## Why I love this Series:
- It perfectly captures summer vibes and growing up.
- The love triangle storyline is emotional and relatable.
- The characters go through real personal growth and challenges.
- The soundtrack makes every scene feel cinematic and meaningful.

### What it is about:
The series follows **Belly**, a girl who spends every summer at Cousins Beach with the Fisher family. As she turns sixteen, everything starts to change — friendships, family, and love. She finds herself caught between brothers Conrad and Jeremiah, and she discovers who she really wants to become.

> “Some summers change your life forever.”
